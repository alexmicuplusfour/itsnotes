@Conclave

Ralph Fiennes doing what Ralph Fiennes does — containment, controlled doubt, an entire interior life expressed through very small gestures. the film looks extraordinary. every frame of the conclave process is deliberate: the red robes against stone corridors, the rituals that are both absurd and genuinely moving.

the ending is apparently divisive. I didn't find it divisive. I found it the only honest place the film could land given what it had been building toward.

what it's actually about: not Catholicism specifically. institutions. the people who believe in them enough to be broken by them, and the people who don't and are broken by something else.

worth seeing on a large screen if you can.
